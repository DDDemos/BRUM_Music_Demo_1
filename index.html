The Sound of Birmingham

Heavy metal was born on these streets. So was the New Romantic pop of the early ’80s, a home-grown reggae scene, and some of Britain’s most ambitious orchestral rock. From Aston to Handsworth to the Rum Runner club on Broad Street, Birmingham has spent sixty years exporting sound — heavier, glossier, and stranger than anywhere else in the country.
