Steel Pulse

Formed
1975
Origin
Handsworth, Birmingham
Genre
Roots Reggae

Formed in 1975 in Handsworth, Birmingham, Steel Pulse emerged from the same Afro-Caribbean community that shaped the city's reggae and punk crossover scenes. Their sharp, socially conscious lyrics addressing racism and inequality made them pioneers of British roots reggae, and in 1986 they became the first British reggae act to win a Grammy Award.

Lineup

  • David Hinds — vocals, guitar
  • Basil Gabbidon — guitar
  • Ronald McQueen — bass
